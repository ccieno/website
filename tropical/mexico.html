What to expect

You'll fly into Cancún and drive south. Playa del Carmen is the liveliest base, Tulum the most photogenic, and Riviera Maya proper is where most of the big beach resorts sit — quiet stretches of sand with not much around them, which is exactly the point for some people and exactly the problem for others. Tell us which camp you're in.

Inland, Chichén Itzá and Cobá are the headline ruins, but the cenotes are the thing people come home talking about: freshwater sinkholes in the jungle, cold and clear, and you can swim in most of them.
