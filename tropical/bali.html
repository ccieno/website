Bali

Almost everyone splits Bali in two: a few nights inland at Ubud among the rice terraces and temples, then the rest of the trip on a beach in the south. It's a good formula and we'd rarely talk you out of it.

Speak to a Bali specialist

What to expect

Getting there takes a stop — usually Doha, Dubai or Singapore — and around seventeen hours door to gate. Once you land, the island is small enough that nothing is more than a couple of hours' drive, though Bali traffic has a way of making two hours feel like four.

Ubud is jungle, temples, and a genuinely excellent food scene. Seminyak is beach clubs and boutiques; Nusa Dua is calmer and better for families; Uluwatu sits on cliffs with the best surf and the worst beach access. Which one suits you is the conversation worth having before you book anything.
