Indian Ocean

Maldives

Twenty-six atolls, a thousand-odd islands, and roughly one resort per island. The hard part isn't finding somewhere lovely — it's picking which one.

Speak to a Maldives specialist

What to expect

Most Maldives holidays follow the same shape: a ten-and-a-half hour flight into Malé, then a speedboat or seaplane out to your island. Once you're there you don't really leave — the island is the resort, and everything from the dive centre to the restaurant is a two-minute walk.

The reefs are the reason to go. On a good house-reef island you can be snorkelling over reef sharks and turtles within thirty seconds of leaving your deck, no boat required. If that matters to you, tell us — house reef quality varies enormously between resorts and it's the single thing we get asked to check most often.
